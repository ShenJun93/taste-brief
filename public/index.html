                            How it works

                            1. Qloo first. The server asks Qloo for places in your city ranked by the taste of people living in the visitor city, and the same lists without that signal. The difference is what that market adds.
                            2. What sets their favourites apart. Tags (ambience, setting, offerings) clearly more common among the market's favourites than among the city's top places.
                            3. An agent maps your ideas. NVIDIA Nemotron calls Qloo tools to find the tag for each idea, then scores it against the same lists — places, music or TV. It can only use tag ids Qloo returned.
                            4. A plan that cites its evidence. The model writes from a fact sheet; actions without a valid citation, actions that push an idea the data does not support, and names that are not in the data are removed. Names and numbers on the page come from Qloo results.
                            5. Also an MCP server. The same tools are served at /mcp for any agent to call.

                            Qloo results describe the aggregate taste of people in a city. They say nothing about any individual guest, and no personal data is sent to Qloo.
