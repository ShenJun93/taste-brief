                        How it works

                        1. Qloo first. The server asks Qloo for places of your kind in your city ranked by the taste of people living in the visitor city, and the same list without that signal. The difference is what the market adds.
                        2. What sets their favourites apart. Tags (ambience, setting, offerings) that appear more often among the market's favourites than city-wide.
                        3. An agent maps your ideas. NVIDIA Nemotron calls Qloo tools to find the tag for each idea, then scores it against the same two lists. It can only use tag ids Qloo returned.
                        4. A plan that cites its evidence. The model writes from a fact sheet; any action without a valid citation is dropped. Names and numbers on this page come from Qloo results, not from the model.
                        5. Also an MCP server. The same tools are served at /mcp for any agent to call.

                        Qloo results describe the aggregate taste of people in a city. They say nothing about any individual guest, and no personal data is sent to Qloo.
